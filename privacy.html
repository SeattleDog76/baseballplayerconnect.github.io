Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Baseball Player Connect handles information when you use the Baseball Player Connect application and related services.

Information We Collect

We may collect information you provide when creating and using an account, such as account information, player or coach profile information, baseball-related details, organization information, game information, availability, and information used to help match players with game opportunities.

Location Information

Baseball Player Connect may use location-related information to support distance-based searches and help users find relevant players or games within a selected search area. The app's location features are intended to support matching and search functionality.

How We Use Information

Information is used to provide and operate the service, create and maintain accounts and profiles, display appropriate game and player opportunities, process invitations and requests, improve functionality, maintain security, and provide support.

Information Sharing

Information necessary to use the service may be visible to other users as part of player profiles, coach profiles, organizations, games, invitations, requests, and matching features. We do not sell personal information.

Service Providers

We may use third-party service providers to host, operate, secure, and support the application. Those providers may process information as necessary to provide their services.

Data Retention and Security

We retain information as reasonably necessary to operate the service, meet legal obligations, resolve disputes, and protect the service. We use reasonable safeguards designed to protect information, but no electronic system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Children and Youth Baseball

Baseball Player Connect is designed for the baseball community, which may include information relating to youth players. Parents, guardians, coaches, and organizations should use the service responsibly and avoid posting unnecessary sensitive information.

Your Choices

You may update certain account or profile information through the app. For privacy questions or requests concerning your information, contact us using the support address below.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The current version will be posted on this page with its effective date.

Contact

For privacy questions or support, email baseballplayerconnect@gmail.com.
